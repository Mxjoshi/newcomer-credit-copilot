## Caveats, stated next to the numbers

- **Hallucination = zero invented numbers or rule ids.** The grounding validator enforces this.
  Separately, the manual rubric pass (`manual-rubric-pass.md`) flags four explanations (GT-11,
  GT-12, GT-13, GT-15) that overstate a refer-severity rule as a "hard stop". Those are
  wording-precision issues, not invented facts, and are tracked there rather than hidden.
- **False approval is zero on a small base.** The meaningful denominator is the 8 decline-labelled
  cases, so this is 0 of 8: a 0% point estimate with a wide upper confidence bound on n=8. Carried
  as a small-sample caveat in `iteration-plan.md`.
- **Refer rate of 41.7% is a property of the engineered set, not a production rate.** The ground
  truth is an even 8/8/8 split by construction (`ground-truth.md`), so one third refers by design.
  The real-world refer rate against the about-15% review-capacity assumption in
  `../01-scope-and-research/deliverables/03-success-metrics.md` is untested, and is an open item in
  `iteration-plan.md`.

## How the explanation was produced (the grounding mechanism at work)

| Outcome | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| grounded on first attempt | 24/24 | the model wrote a fully grounded explanation first time |
| passed on retry | 0/24 | the first draft had an ungrounded item, the validator caught it, the retry was clean |
| fell back to template | 0/24 | both attempts failed grounding, the deterministic template was shown |

In every case the officer-facing explanation was fully grounded; the validator guarantees no
ungrounded text reaches the officer. The hallucination metric is therefore enforced, not only
measured.

## Note on the four decision mismatches

The four accuracy mismatches are the same band-driven boundary cases the deterministic harness
reports (GT-08, GT-12, GT-16, GT-21). They are surfaced, not tuned away (decision M5): the
scorecard tiers were set from domain rationale, independently of and without tuning to the locked
labels, so a mismatch is information about a borderline case, not a bug to hide. All four are
reasonable refers or a conservative call, none is a false approval.
